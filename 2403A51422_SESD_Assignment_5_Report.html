1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

This assignment report delivers a comprehensive Agile/Scrum simulation for Case Study 1: Online Food Ordering App from the official SESD Assignment 5 specification. The project designs and delivers a full-stack solution called FoodSwift, allowing campus students and faculty to browse restaurants, explore categorized menus, manage a shopping cart, simulate digital payments, and track orders through a live 5-stage progression stepper.
